\begin{abstract}
We evaluate classical truncated singular-value decomposition for top-$N$
MovieLens recommendation under a strict global temporal split. The system
includes point-in-time features, strong non-neural baselines, reproducible
Floci/AWS infrastructure, and an offline-to-online parity test.
\end{abstract}
