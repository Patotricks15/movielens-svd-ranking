\section{Experimental Setup}
We report Recall@10, MRR@10, catalogue coverage, and
\[
\mathrm{NDCG}@K=\frac{1}{|\mathcal U|}\sum_u
\frac{\sum_{j=1}^K(2^{rel_{uj}}-1)/\log_2(j+1)}
{\mathrm{IDCG}_u@K}.
\]
Candidates are the full catalogue minus training history. Hyperparameters are
selected on validation only. Synthetic data is included for CI; real
MovieLens data is downloaded on demand and is never committed.
