\section{Feature Engineering}
The declarative catalogue in \texttt{conf/features.yaml} covers user activity,
rating statistics, movie popularity, age, genre count, recency, and
time-decayed genre affinity. A prediction at time $t$ uses only events with
$t_{ui}<t$. Time-decayed affinity is
\[
a_{uc}(t)=\sum_{j:t_{uj}<t}\mathbb{1}[c_j=c]
          \exp[-\lambda(t-t_{uj})].
\]
