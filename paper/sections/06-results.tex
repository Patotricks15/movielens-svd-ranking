\section{Deployment}
Training writes factors and feature tables to S3 and publishes precomputed
top-$N$ lists to DynamoDB. Lambda performs a bounded key lookup. EventBridge
schedules retraining and CloudWatch receives logs. The default Terraform
profile targets Floci/LocalStack and therefore requires no paid AWS account.
