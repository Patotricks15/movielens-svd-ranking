\section{Methodology}
Let $X\in\{0,1\}^{|\mathcal U|\times|\mathcal I|}$ be the sparse training
interaction matrix. PureSVD computes the rank-$k$ approximation
\[
X\approx U_k\Sigma_kV_k^\top
\]
that minimizes $\|X-\hat X\|_F$ among rank-$k$ matrices. The score for user
$u$ and item $i$ is
\[
\hat y_{ui}=(U_k)_{u:}\Sigma_k(V_k)_{:i}.
\]
Items in the user's training history are removed before top-$N$ selection.
Unseen users receive the popularity ordering. Sparse Lanczos SVD avoids
materializing the full decomposition.

The strongest baseline uses shrunk cosine:
\[
s(i,j)=\frac{X_{:i}^{\top}X_{:j}}
{\|X_{:i}\|_2\|X_{:j}\|_2+\beta}.
\]
