\section{Dataset and Split}
MovieLens ratings contain user, movie, rating, and Unix timestamp fields.
Ratings of at least four define positive ranking interactions. An iterative
five-core removes entities without enough evidence. If $\tau_1$ and $\tau_2$
are the 80th and 90th timestamp percentiles, respectively, training uses
$t<\tau_1$, validation uses $\tau_1\le t<\tau_2$, and test uses
$t\ge\tau_2$. All transformations are fitted on training data only.
